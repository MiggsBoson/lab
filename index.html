The lab

Small experiments. Try them, break them, tell us what to build next.

  • Jam One beat for everyone. Tap squares to change it; everyone hears the same loop at the same moment.
  • Fridge magnets One fridge door, a hundred words, everyone dragging them at once.
  • Pass it on Read the note the last stranger left you. Leave one for the next.
  • Tug of war Red against blue. Pick a side and hammer the button.
  • The plant One plant, everyone's watering can. Keep it alive, or watch it wilt.
  • Chess One board for everyone. Make the first move to play white; the next person plays black.
  • Live data Weather, the space station right now, a random dog and Reddit's top post of the day.
  • Playground Counters, dice, coins and colours to poke at.
  • Hello The first experiment: proof that the lab works.
